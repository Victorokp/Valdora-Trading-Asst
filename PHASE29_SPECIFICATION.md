## 1. Purpose

Try to break the Golden Reference by subjecting it to realistic
perturbations and adverse assumptions. The output is a **robustness
envelope**, not a new strategy. No parameter may be tuned toward
profitability; every experiment is pre-registered in
`PHASE29_EXPERIMENT_REGISTRY.md` before execution.

## 4. Experiment families (all pre-registered)

- **A — Execution stress**: friction grid {0, 1.5*, 3, 4, 5, 6} pips;
  adverse-only entry slippage {0.5, 1.0, 2.0} pips on top of control friction.
- **B — Entry delay**: entry delayed 1 and 2 bars after signal (signal set,
  brackets, and all other semantics unchanged).
- **C — Exit execution degradation**: adverse exit slippage {0.5, 1.0} pips
  (stop side and target side), gap exits filled at the gap-through level
  instead of the actual open.
- **D — Parameter perturbation** (one-at-a-time, no combinations, no
  selection): stop 0.9/1.1 ATR; target 1.8/2.2 ATR; daily EMA20→19/21;
  daily EMA50→49/51; weekly EMA10→9/11; weekly EMA20→19/21.
- **E — Signal timing**: signal series shifted 1 day later (valid); 1-day
  earlier variant **NOT VALID — WOULD INTRODUCE LOOK-AHEAD → NOT RUN**.
- **F — Data/trade omission**: remove top-1/2/5/10 winners; random omission
  1/5/10% × 20 seeds (29092601–29092620); remove 10%/20% of winners only.
- **G — Trade-order stress**: 10,000 permutations, seed 20290926.
- **H — Exposure stress**: concurrency caps 3 / 2 / 1 (descriptive; not
  candidates); concurrency statistics of the control ledger.
- **I — Capital path / drawdown envelope**: episode depths, recovery
  lengths, drawdown-threshold counts, MC order envelope.
- **J — Temporal fragility**: all 16 walk-forward test windows.
- **K — Regime fragility**: volatility cohorts (ATR/price < 0.5%, 0.5–1.0%,
  > 1.0%) and trend-width terciles (daily EMA20−EMA50 gap), entry-time
  information only, descriptive.
- **L — Cross-pair (secondary)**: unchanged Golden logic on GBPUSD, USDJPY,
  AUDUSD; full-history and OOS-2010–2025 slices, reported independently,
  never pooled.

## 5. Interpretation scale (pre-registered)

HOLDS / DEGRADED-BUT-POSITIVE / FRAGILE / BREAKS — definitions in the
registry; failure criteria per family in the registry tables. No threshold
was invented or adjusted after seeing results.
